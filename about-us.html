ABOUT ASKNETIC

A diversified Tanzanian group, built on trust.

Established in 2023 in Dar es Salaam, working across seven sectors with one standard of service.

Asknetic Enterprises Ltd was established in 2023 as a group of companies in Tanzania, located in the commercial capital of Dar es Salaam, with the intention of securing lucrative business and investment opportunities within Tanzania.

We strive to provide products, services and brands that go well with our customers' choices. We provide a range of services spanning medical services, car hire, agro crop, ICT and digital solutions, building maintenance, real estate, general supplies, and travel agency and visa assistance — to producing products and brands that are safe for the environment as well as the consumer's health, with an exceptional service experience.

We pride ourselves on employing the best and brightest individuals, utilising the highest quality materials and the latest methods, to ensure our customers receive the level of quality and value they deserve.

Mission

Become essential to our customers by providing differentiated products and services to help them achieve their aspirations, and for us to be the best-in-class service provider in Africa.

Vision

To become a prominent and diverse company with excellent service across all our core businesses.

With our vision we will always strive harder in ensuring specialised solutions, so as to be a market leader and an epitome company. Having established a worldwide network of partnerships, we've ensured improved quality and service in line with our core values.
